# Beacon

**Beacon** is the open-source grader [[Agentic Fabriq]] uses to score [[mnemiq]] and other [[Text-to-SQL]] systems on public benchmarks such as [[BIRD]] and [[Spider 2.0]], supporting both exact-match and got-the-facts grading rules.
