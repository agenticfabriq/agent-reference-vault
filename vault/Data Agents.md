# Data Agents

**Data agents** are AI agents that reason over and act on an organization's databases. They face two problems at once: accuracy ([[Text-to-SQL]] against real schemas) and governance (what the agent may read and for whom). See [[mnemiq]] and [[Fabriq Enterprise]].
